# 📊 Sales Analytics & Business Intelligence Dashboard — Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end **Sales Analytics and Business Intelligence solution developed in Microsoft Power BI** 
to analyze sales performance across products, customers, regions, profitability, returns, and customer segments.

The dashboard transforms sales data into an interactive analytical report that enables business users to monitor key performance indicators,
identify sales trends, compare actual performance against targets, analyze regional performance, and understand customer purchasing behavior.

The report is structured into **four analytical pages**, each designed for a specific business perspective:

1. **Executive Sales Dashboard**
2. **Geographical / Regional Analysis**
3. **Product Performance & Target Analysis**
4. **Customer Analytics**

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to provide a centralized view of business performance and answer important business questions such as:

* How is overall revenue and profit performing?
* What is the total order volume?
* What percentage of orders are returned?
* How are revenue, profit, orders, and returns changing over time?
* Which product categories generate the highest order volume?
* Which products generate the most revenue?
* Which products have higher return rates?
* How are orders distributed across different countries/regions?
* Is monthly performance meeting the defined targets?
* How does customer income level affect order volume?
* Which occupation groups contribute the most orders?
* Who are the highest-value customers?
* How is the customer base changing over time?
* What is the average revenue generated per customer?

# 📑 Dashboard Structure

## 1️⃣ Executive Sales Dashboard

The first page is designed as the main management-level dashboard.

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard displays four primary KPIs:

* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Profit**
* **Total Orders**
* **Return Rate**

These KPIs provide an immediate overview of business performance.

### Weekly Revenue Trend

A weekly revenue line chart is used to analyze revenue movement over time.

The visualization helps identify:

* Revenue growth patterns
* Periods of increased/decreased sales
* Long-term sales trends
* Changes in business performance over time

### Orders by Product Category

The dashboard compares order volume across major product categories, including:

* Accessories
* Bikes
* Clothing

This provides a quick view of category-level demand.

### Product Performance Matrix

A detailed matrix provides product-level analysis using:

* Product Name
* Total Orders
* Total Revenue
* Return Rate

This allows users to compare individual products across sales volume, revenue contribution and return behavior.

### Monthly Performance Cards

The dashboard also contains monthly performance indicators for:

* Monthly Revenue
* Monthly Orders
* Monthly Returns

Each metric is presented with a comparison against the previous month to make short-term performance changes easier to identify.

### Product Highlights

The executive dashboard also highlights:

* Most Ordered Product Type
* Most Returned Product Type

These visuals help identify products with particularly high purchase or return activity.

---

## 2️⃣ Geographical / Regional Analysis

The second page focuses on the geographical distribution of sales.

### Interactive Country Slicer

Users can filter the analysis by country, including:

* Australia
* Canada
* France
* Germany
* United Kingdom
* United States

### Geographical Sales Map

An interactive map visualizes order distribution geographically.

Bubble size represents the relative order volume for each location.

This enables users to:

* Compare order activity between countries
* Identify high-volume markets
* Identify lower-volume markets
* Understand geographical concentration of sales

The map can be interactively filtered using the country slicer, allowing users to perform focused regional analysis.

---

## 3️⃣ Product Performance & Target Analysis

The third page focuses on product-level performance and target achievement.

### Product Filter

An interactive **Product Name slicer** allows users to select a specific product and analyze its performance.

This makes the page useful for detailed product-level investigation.

### Performance vs Target

Three gauge visuals compare current monthly performance against defined targets:

* **Monthly Orders vs Target**
* **Monthly Revenue vs Target**
* **Monthly Profit vs Target**

These visuals help stakeholders quickly understand whether actual performance is approaching or meeting the expected target.

### Weekly Profit Trend

A weekly profit area/line visualization tracks profitability over time.

This helps identify:

* Profit growth
* Periods of weaker profitability
* Changes in profitability trends
* Overall business performance progression

### Weekly Returns Trend

A weekly returns visualization tracks return activity over time.

This helps identify periods where return activity increases and provides an additional perspective alongside sales and profit trends.

---

## 4️⃣ Customer Analytics

The fourth page focuses on customer behavior, segmentation and customer value.

### Customer KPIs

The dashboard highlights:

* **Total Customers:** 17.4K
* **Revenue per Customer:** ₹1.4K

These KPIs provide an overview of customer scale and average customer value.

### Weekly Customer Trend

A weekly customer line chart tracks customer activity over time.

This helps identify changes in customer engagement and overall customer growth patterns.

### Orders by Income Level

A donut chart analyzes order distribution across different customer income segments:

* Low
* Average
* High
* Very High

This provides insight into how purchasing activity is distributed across income groups.

### Orders by Occupation Level

Another donut chart analyzes order distribution across occupation groups such as:

* Professional
* Skilled Manual
* Management
* Clerical
* Manual

This provides a demographic perspective on customer purchasing behavior.

### Customer Detail Table

A detailed customer table contains:

* Customer Key
* Customer Name
* Total Orders
* Revenue

This allows users to move from high-level customer analysis to individual customer performance.

### Top Customer Analysis

The dashboard identifies the **Top Customer by Revenue** and displays:

* Customer Name
* Total Orders
* Total Revenue

This provides a quick way to identify high-value customers and understand their contribution to the business.
